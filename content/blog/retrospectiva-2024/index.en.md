## Introduction

In the last few years I realized that remembering everything I lived through in a year had become a challenge for me. My mind easily recalled things related to studies or work, but for events outside that context, I often had to dig deep to remember them. I believe this made me a bit ungrateful to myself, always focused on the finish line without paying much attention to the journey I was living.

That's why, this year, I decided to start writing a retrospective to reinforce my whole path so far and remember the moments I lived, the challenges I overcame and the experiences I shared with my family and friends.

While drafting this post, going through the year's photos and remembering those moments, I'd say it was quite a year :] .

## Reflection

At the end of the year I usually get pretty reflective about my life, the things I did or didn't do, the uncertainties of the coming years, my dreams and my purpose in life.

I've always been very demanding of myself, pushing hard not to fail because I knew there were people who needed me and counted on me. I believe this shaped me in a way, but today I realize it's essential to find balance in life. There are times when focus can lead us to dedicate more to studies or work, but there are also situations where **"hitting the brakes"** can be the best option!

> That's why keeping balance in life is essential!

## My year

This year I decided to be more grateful to myself and try to live in a lighter, more balanced way, especially when it comes to my work routine.

I admit I still have a lot to adjust lol, but I believe this year was important to let the **"dust settle"** and reflect on my path so far. I understood that, given the variables life gave me, things happened exactly as they should. That each person's starting point is unique, and that a life without goals and purpose can lead us to a tireless urge to do things that, many times, take us nowhere.

Finally, I also learned that the small wins along the way deserve to be celebrated just as much as the finish line.

## Travel

This year, like every other year, I went to the beach many times. I think it's a place where I can connect with nature and recharge. Who knows, maybe in 2025 I'll start surfing 👀 lol.

Still on nature, I went back to **Visconde de Mauá** with my sister. We were very lucky on this trip, because a few days before we arrived there was [very heavy rain in the region](https://g1.globo.com/rj/sul-do-rio-costa-verde/noticia/2024/02/09/temporal-provoca-tromba-dagua-e-alaga-casas-em-visconde-de-maua.ghtml), flooding parts of the town. Fortunately, the weather improved and we got to enjoy the waterfalls in the area. If you want to venture out there, I recommend bringing comfortable sneakers and a good repellent to enjoy the trails and waterfalls. It's the ideal destination for anyone looking for contact with nature in a more *rustic* style. The locals are amazing and super welcoming, the food is very good and cheap, and the Minas cheese and dulce de leche are second to none. It's well worth a visit!

Another very memorable trip for me was taking my mom to **Campos do Jordão** for the first time. She had dreamed of visiting the town since she was a child, and making this dream come true, letting her enjoy it without worries, was simply unforgettable for me. Next year I want to do more of this with her.

A few months later, after a lot of planning and many hours on YouTube watching travel tips, I finally traveled to **Florida** and visited **Disney** for the first time with my wife. It was a dream come true for both of us! I believe the child inside us can come back to life there. The experience and immersion the parks offer are amazing. However, unlucky as I am lol, I caught a very bad flu on the very first day and only got better almost two weeks later 🫠. On top of that, we went through two hurricanes, [Helene and Milton](https://www.bbc.com/weather/articles/cq64vnj51p1o). For anyone who has never been through one it's pretty strange, but since hurricanes are very common there, there's a whole structure in place to keep people safe. Fortunately it passed quickly and in the end everything turned out fine 🙏. Even with so many emotions while I was there, it was one of the best trips of my life; it deserves a post of its own.

And to close the year, as a good beer lover, I went to **Oktoberfest** 🍺 in **Blumenau** for the **5th** time. I think going to the festival every year has become a ritual, and there's more in 2025 hahaha.

## Concerts

I'd say this was the year of concerts, lol. In total there were **4 concerts** and my first festival at **Lollapalooza**. Getting to see bands I listened to as a teenager was surreal, pure nostalgia and an experience I'll carry for life. Bands like **Natiruts**, **Capital Inicial**, **Forfun**, **Blink-182**, **Limp Bizkit**, **The Offspring**, **Kings of Leon**, **Thirty Seconds to Mars** and, of course, my favorite international band, **Linkin Park** 🙌!

Unfortunately, I never got the chance to see the old lineup, but watching the band's comeback was insane, and Emily is simply incredible on vocals.

## Studies

A very important milestone for me was finishing my **MBA in Cloud and Architecture**, especially after so many years studying on my own since graduating in **2014**. Even though cloud isn't part of my day-to-day, learning more about it and following how things have evolved was essential to keep me up to date with tech industry trends. I'd say that now I'm no longer so afraid of putting my credit card into the AWS console and ending up with an absurd bill at the end of the month 🤑 lol.

## Work

For a few years now, I've been working hard to grow as a professional and take the next step in my career. Along the way, I went through many rounds of interviews, talking to people with different skills, from managers to staff and principal engineers, gathering feedback about my strengths and the areas I needed to develop. A lot happened during this process, but I believe these experiences were essential to help me mature as a professional over the last few years.

Reflecting on the **“other side of the coin”** was key to finding ways to grow and understanding that things happen when they're truly meant to happen. It may sound a bit obvious, I know, but this moment of reflection reminded me a lot of the speech **Steve Jobs** gave at Stanford University's commencement in 2005 about **“connecting the dots”**:

> “You can’t connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards. So you have to trust that the dots will somehow connect in your future.”

And finally, in early December, I had the opportunity to start as a **Staff Engineer** at **PicPay**. I know the challenges will be huge and that I have a lot to learn and grow as a professional, but I believe this achievement came at the most opportune moment, and I'm very excited about next year's challenges 🙌.

## Goals for 2025

Looking back at my 2024 goals, I realized I didn't reach some of them, and **that's okay** 😌. But I see that I neglected one of the most important pillars, **taking care of my health**. That's something I really want to improve in **2025**.

So, as goals for next year, I hope to:

- Take care of my health ❤️‍🩹;
- Donate blood 🩸;
- Build a healthy exercise routine 🏋;
- Pick up a new hobby 🏄‍♂️;
- Run the São Silvestre race 🏃‍♂️;
- Improve my English 🇺🇸;
- Read 4 books 📚;
- Contribute to the tech community 🤖.

But something I won't forget during these 365 days…goals are important, having clear methods and objectives is necessary, and so is dedication to studies and work, but understanding where I am in life and taking breaks to recharge, reflect, adjust course and move forward will make a big difference.

**Life is finite, people come and go, so don't be so hard on yourself, don't compare yourself to others, get inspired! And above all, enjoy the journey 😉.**

Here's to a great **2025**, let's go 🤘!!!
