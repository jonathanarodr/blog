Welcome to my personal blog! This is the first post, and I'm excited to start sharing knowledge, projects and reflections about software development, plus a few other topics along the way.

<!--more-->

## Inspiration

I created this blog because I wanted a space of my own to write freely, build my portfolio, share what I learn and give back to the community that was so important at the start of my career, while also serving as a digital diary for myself.

## How these posts are written

Every post here is written and reviewed by me. I use AI as a collaborator: to discuss ideas, check facts, tighten the structure and catch mistakes, the same way I'd lean on a good editor or a colleague's code review.

What I won't do is publish for the sake of publishing. No generated filler, no posts written to chase algorithms, no slop. If something is here, it's because I had something worth saying, I tried what I describe, and I stand behind it.

## What you'll find here

This blog documents my journey as a developer and shares what I learn with the community. The topics will vary, for example:

- **Technical tutorials**: Practical guides about technologies and tools I'm studying.
- **Personal projects**: A showcase of my projects and experiments.
- **Reflections**: Thoughts about software development and about life 😅.
