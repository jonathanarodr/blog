## Introduction

**Mone** is a personal finance app for anyone who wants to take control of their money without the hassle. You record what comes in and what goes out, organize your accounts and keep track of your finances in a simple way, right on your phone.

## Features

- **Bank accounts**: add your accounts and manage them all in one place.
- **Income and expenses**: record every transaction and see where your money is going.
- **Pending transactions**: get alerts for transactions that are still pending, so nothing slips through.

## How it started

Mone started in 2018 as the final (capstone) project of Udacity's Android Nanodegree. After a few years on hold, it came back as a side project, and the first version reached Google Play at the end of 2021. Today it's the app I use to manage my own finances, and it keeps evolving little by little. I told this story in the post [Mone, the challenges of my first app](/en/blog/mone-the-challenges-of-my-first-app/).

## Under the hood

- Native Android app, written in Kotlin.
- Screen design and prototypes in Figma.
- Authentication, contextual remote configs, analytics and NoSQL data modeling.
